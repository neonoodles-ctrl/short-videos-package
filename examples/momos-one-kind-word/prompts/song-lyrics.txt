[Verse 1]
Momo stays up late tonight.
One small lamp is shining bright.
One blanket lies on a shelf.
Momo wraps it round herself.

[Verse 2]
One small cushion sits alone.
Momo holds it as her own.
One wide chair waits in the glow.
Momo rocks herself so slow.

[Verse 3]
One thick quilt lies on a seat.
Momo tucks it round her feet.
Momo helps her friends all day.
Now she helps herself this way.

[Outro]
Momo takes her time to rest.
Slow and soft is what is best.
One warm word stays in her head.
'You are kind.' Then off to bed.
